ABOUT HSCR

Built in Zambia, for the way Zambian venues actually run.

Most table-service technology assumes stable venue Wi-Fi, a data bundle, and power that never cuts out. In Zambia, none of those can be counted on — so we built the opposite: a watch that creates its own network and needs nothing from the venue at all.

Founded to make good service possible everywhere from a Lusaka bistro to a Livingstone lodge, without asking the owner to first solve their internet.

ADVANTAGES

Reliable where imported solutions aren't

Fully offline

Works straight through load-shedding and dead zones — nothing to connect to but the watch itself.

Nothing to install

Guests use the browser already on their phone. Joining takes one QR scan — no app, no account.

Impossible to miss

A repeating vibration and an on-screen table number — not a beep across a noisy room.

Built to be affordable

Off-the-shelf electronics and a sunlight-readable e-paper screen keep unit cost low and battery life long.

Locally supported

Set up, spares and support from a team on the ground — not a call centre in another time zone.

One watch, one table

Simple to reason about for staff: each watch belongs to a table, and shows exactly what that table needs.
